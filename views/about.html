Prime Location

Situated in a prestigious area of Atrau in Kazakhstan, this development offers residents seamless access to key business districts, upscale dining, shopping, and essential services—combining convenience with exclusivity.

Modern Design

The two-building complex showcases contemporary architecture with thoughtfully designed studio, 2-bedroom, and 3-bedroom apartments, featuring high-quality finishes, efficient layouts, and abundant natural light.

Sustainable Living

Built with a focus on long-term efficiency, the project incorporates energy-conscious materials and systems to reduce environmental impact while enhancing comfort and lowering utility costs for residents.

The Vision

Atrau City was born from a simple ambition: to raise the standard of urban living in one of Kazakhstan's most dynamic cities. At a time when Atrau is growing rapidly as a regional economic hub, this project offers residents a modern, comfortable home that reflects the city's momentum.

The development brings together two contemporary residential buildings, thoughtfully designed to balance private living with vibrant ground-floor commercial spaces. Every detail — from the apartment layouts to the shared environment — has been considered with the future resident in mind.

Atrau City is more than a building. It is an investment in a lifestyle, and in the future of a city on the rise.

The Builder

Aktau Center Stroy (ТОО Актау Центр Строй) is a Kazakhstani construction and real estate development company founded in 2004. With over 20 years of experience and more than 50 completed projects across Aktau and Atyrau, the company has established itself as one of western Kazakhstan's most active developers.

Operating as a full turnkey contractor — from design through to handover — Aktau Center Stroy has delivered a wide range of projects including multi-storey residential complexes, hotel developments, administrative buildings, and business centres. Notable completed projects include Golden Towers, a 19-floor elite residential complex in Aktau, and Caspian City, a multi-building complex located 800 metres from the Caspian Sea shore.
