Atrau City

Kazakhstan

Prime Location

Situated in a prestigious area of Atrau in Kazakhstan, this development offers residents seamless access to key business districts, upscale dining, shopping, and essential services—combining convenience with exclusivity.

Modern Design

The two-building complex showcases contemporary architecture with thoughtfully designed studio, 2-bedroom, and 3-bedroom apartments, featuring high-quality finishes, efficient layouts, and abundant natural light.

Sustainable Living

Built with a focus on long-term efficiency, the project incorporates energy-conscious materials and systems to reduce environmental impact while enhancing comfort and lowering utility costs for residents.
